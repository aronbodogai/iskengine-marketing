Opportunity feed

opportunity feed screenshot

All buildable T1 items in one ranked list. Default sort is ISK/day per slot; switch to profit/run, margin %, or profit at achievable daily volume. Filter by market group, min margin, max capital, max build time, or owned-blueprints-only. Illiquid items are hidden, not just pushed down.

Full cost & revenue breakdown

opportunity detail screenshot

Line-by-line materials (qty, unit price, total, m³), job install cost, facility tax, and hauling when input and output markets differ. Revenue in two modes: patient (list a sell order, net of broker fee and sales tax) and instant (fill buy orders, net of sales tax). Net profit and margin for both.

Your real fit

personal fit screenshot

Log in with EVE SSO (read blueprints + read skills, nothing else). The engine uses your actual owned blueprints with real ME/TE and BPC runs, and your skills for accurate broker fee, sales tax, and job durations. Don't own the blueprint? It falls back to ME10/TE20, adjustable per item and globally, and always flags the assumption.

Region data on demand

market context screenshot

Pick an input market (materials priced here) and an output market (product sold here) — each a region, optionally narrowed to a station. One-click "price inputs from Jita." Only selected regions are ingested; warm regions refresh at ESI cadence (~5 min), history daily, and go cold after 7 days unused. Reads the full book of any player structure a linked character can dock at.

Time-to-sell model

liquidity panel screenshot

Daily volume is the trailing 30-day median (median, not mean, to resist spikes). TTS = quantity ÷ (daily volume × capture share), with an adjustable default capture share of 10%. Patient mode adds a queue penalty from the sell wall ahead of your listing price. Regional-history limitations are disclosed right next to the figure.

Every number is auditable

expandable math screenshot

Reference prices are robust — a volume-weighted percentile of the order book, never a single best order, outliers excluded. Every displayed value is net of fees, carries a freshness timestamp per data source, and expands to show the exact math. No figure without a visible source or assumption.
